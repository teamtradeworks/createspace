# Meta ads: STEM range (4:5 feed)

Scope: three static Meta feed ads, 1080x1350, evergreen range awareness. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: SA parents of STEM-curious kids. Action: tap through to shop by category.
Proof: real lifestyle and product photography from assets/product; real facts only (six categories, ages 3 to 13+, free delivery over R1,500). No discount.
Constraints: brand world is fixed (navy/white grounds, accent palette, Outfit, CREATESPACE logo). User asked for all three versions, so all three dealt structures are built rather than one locked.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Show the range as things a child does, not a catalogue grid. Refuses the default "logo + collage + Shop Now" sale layout of the previous Makerzoid ad.
OWN-WORLD: Navy #0C1446 and white grounds; each category owns one accent (blue coding, yellow circuits, orange building, green earth, red space, purple chemistry); Outfit 600 display, 400 body; real photos only.
STORY: A parent sees six concrete activities, recognises their child in one, and taps to shop that kind of kit.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A (lead) week planner: headline "Robots on Monday. Rockets by Friday." over six day rows with photo, verb, category. B: photo row, navy headline band "What kind of curious is yours?", photo row, each photo tagged with a kid type. C: white specimen board, six product objects with leader-line labels, headline "Pick a science. Any science." CTA pill bottom on all three.
FORM: surface structures 7 (week planner), 2 (kid types), 3 (flat-lay) from a ranked list of seven; seed key 04b5fa16.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
