# Hero kits: four single-product concepts (Meta + Google PMax)

Scope: four static ad concepts, each in meta-portrait, meta-square, meta-story, google-landscape, google-square, google-portrait. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: SA parents (core: mothers 35-54) and grandparents (women 55+, gift variant). Action: tap to the product page.
Proof: real product and customer photography; facts from content.md and the live product page (price, age). Evidence: creative-insights-365d-2026-10-02.md (one product per image, number in the headline, age as filter, screen-free tied to a product, sensory detail, gift that promises use).
Constraints: marketing/DESIGN.md wins over the insights doc's visual rules (user decision 2026-10-02): orange CTA with navy label, SemiBold headlines, one or two highlighted words. No kicker above headlines. Google images carry no CTA button and minimal text.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Each ad dramatises the one fact that sold its product in the data, as an object you could hold. Refuses the category default of logo + collage + "Shop now" and the product grid that returned ROAS 0.48.
OWN-WORLD: Night-Sky Workshop (DESIGN.md). Navy ground for three concepts, white ground for the gift concept so it stands apart in feed. One product photo per canvas, 16px radius. Outfit 600 headlines in sentence case, 700 numerals. Orange CTA with navy label. Accent per concept: Human Body green (glow), Makerzoid orange, Tale-Bot blue and green from the robot's own buttons, Air Rockets yellow gift tag.
STORY: A parent sees one kit doing its thing, reads one hard fact (glowing bones, 200 robots, coding before reading, still used in January), sees age and price, taps.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Human Body: headline top, lights-on / lights-off diptych of two tall photo panels, captions under each, price + CTA bottom. Makerzoid: three-line typographic sentence "600 pieces. / 200 robots. / One very busy kid." with numerals in orange at display scale over navy, customer photo of the tray full-width below. Tale-Bot: full-bleed photo of children pressing the buttons, navy scrim, a row of drawn command buttons spelling a programme, headline "Coding before reading." White gift concept: outdoor launch photo top (product and child in shot), a yellow gift tag with a two-item checklist over open grass, headline below. Makerzoid Google: "Build it" and "Code it" photo pair.
FORM: structures 7 (gift tag, lead), 4 (number sentence), 2 (full-bleed scrim) from the ranked list of seven, plus 6 (lights on / lights off diptych) as the fourth concept's own form; seed key 6c3d2206.
