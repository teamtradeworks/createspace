# Email square: Join us at CREATESPACE

Scope: one 1080 x 1080 image (canvas meta-square) placed inside marketing emails, shown at roughly 540 to 600px wide. Persuade mode. Reader: any subscriber (parent-leaning), glancing in an inbox. Action: none on the image itself; it sets a warm, inviting tone for the email around it. Copy is the headline only, "Join us at CREATESPACE". Ground: navy (user choice). Code-led build, no comp.
