# Christmas gift guide: Meta feed portrait

Scope: one fixed 1080 x 1350 Meta feed ad (lead asset); square and story adapt from it. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: parents buying a Christmas gift for their own child, ages 3 to 14. Job: spot a kit that fits their child in a few seconds, tap through to /shop.
Proof/content: five in-stock kits with real ages (campaign brief.md, What we can say). No prices on the image, no sale, no delivery-by-Christmas promise.
Constraints: Navy-or-White ground, Outfit for brand type, orange CTA with navy label, no childish styling, no em dashes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A child's Christmas wish list, already granted: five wishes in their own words, each ticked and answered with the real kit and its age. Refuses the category default of a product grid with a "Gift guide" header.

OWN-WORLD: Deep Space Navy ground with a soft blue and purple glow; one white lined notepad page, slightly tilted, red margin rule; wishes in Caveat navy ink, orange ticks, the real kit name and age set in Outfit beneath each wish; small photo cut-outs taped beside each line with translucent tape; one yellow doodled star. Outfit SemiBold headline, orange CTA.

STORY: The parent recognises their own child's list, sees each wish matched to a real kit at a real age, believes these are gifts that keep being played with, and taps to shop.
