# Surface brief: Christmas, the gift that keeps building (feed portrait, square, story)

Mode: Persuade. Reader: parents scrolling Instagram or Facebook in December, cold and warm audiences. Campaign brief: `campaigns/2026-11-christmas-assembly-required/brief.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: one Christmas gift, followed past the unwrapping. The ad is the instruction sheet from inside the Makerzoid Robot Master box, with real photos as the diagrams. It refuses the category default of a product hero with a price.

OWN-WORLD: Deep Space Navy ground printed like gift wrap: Spark Yellow and white stars scattered irregularly per canvas (never tiled) and kept clear of text. A white kit-manual page, 6px corners, tilted -1 degree, paper drop and blue/purple glow. Manual language: navy step numerals in solid discs, 2px navy-ringed photo frames at 4px corners, orange chevrons between steps, a tracked uppercase sheet title. Outfit only; one orange word in the headline.

STORY: the parent sees "The gift that keeps building.", follows one kit from unwrapping under the tree to building, coding and the next build, learns its name and ages, and taps Shop the kit to the product page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: logo top left; two-line headline with "building." orange; "200+ robots in one box." below; the 2 x 2 step sheet filling the middle; footer with the kit name, ages and free delivery left, orange CTA right (stacked with a full-width CTA on the story).

FORM: instruction sheet, pinned by the campaign brief (no concept-seed roll). Manual-page and full-bleed-photo alternatives were tried on 2026-11-26 and rejected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
