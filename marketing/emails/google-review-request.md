# Google review request email

Sent to customers after they've received an order, asking for a Google review. It goes out from our email tool, not from the repo; this file is the source of truth for the copy. Paste it into the tool when it changes.

The message leans on CREATESPACE being a small, family-run business, so it feels personal without sounding needy.

## Subject

How did we do?

## Preview text

We'd love to hear about your order.

## Body

Hi {{ data.customer.first_name|default:'there' }},

Thank you for shopping with CREATESPACE! We're a small, family-run business, and we love knowing our kits are out there getting kids excited about STEM.

If you have a minute, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review. Tell us how it all went, whether it's the products, our service or the delivery.

Reviews help other parents and educators find us.

**[Leave a Google review]** (button)

Thanks for supporting us,
[Name] and the CREATESPACE family

## Notes

- Replace `[Name]` with a real first name before sending.
- The button links straight to the Google review form. Get the link from Google Business Profile under "Ask for reviews".
- Keep each paragraph on its own line in the email tool.
- Don't offer a discount or reward for reviews. Google's policy doesn't allow incentivised reviews.
