# Design System: CREATESPACE

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night-Sky Workshop"**

CREATESPACE looks like a workbench set up under a clear night sky. Deep Space Navy is the sky: a calm, trustworthy ground that fills heroes, headers, footers and ad canvases. Against it, the brand's toy-bright accents (orange, blue, purple, green, yellow, red) behave like the kit pieces scattered on the bench. They light up single words, price tags, badges and the outlined illustrations of robots, planets, beakers and nuts, but they never take over the ground. The white side of the system is the same workshop with the lights on: clean white and a whisper of grey, navy type, the same accents doing the same jobs.

The feel is friendly and confident rather than loud. Type is one family (Outfit) used almost entirely at SemiBold for headings and Regular for reading, so the personality comes from colour and imagery, not from typographic stunts. Shapes are soft (8px buttons, 16px cards, full pills) and surfaces are mostly flat, with depth coming from soft coloured glows and navy photo scrims rather than heavy shadows. Real photography of children and hands building kits carries the emotional weight; illustrations are the seasoning.

The logo itself sets the tone: a tightly tracked, condensed wordmark where the "A" in SPACE stands apart in Rocket Red and doubles as an upward arrow. Space, lift and exploration are the idea, so layouts leave air around the mark and around the key message.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Navy or white ground, always; accents are highlights, never backgrounds.
- Launch Orange is the single action colour: every primary call to action is orange.
- Rocket Red marks money and urgency: sale pills, discount badges, sale prices.
- One family, Outfit: SemiBold headlines, Regular body, heavier weights only for numbers and badges.
- Soft, friendly corners and pill-shaped labels.
- Flat surfaces lifted by blurred blue and purple glows, not drop shadows.
- Outlined, colourful STEM illustrations used sparingly: faint in the background, or a few full-colour pieces orbiting the hero subject.

## Colors

A deep navy and white pairing carries the brand, with seven toy-bright accents from the CI used as highlights and signals.

### Primary
- **Deep Space Navy** (#0C1446): The brand ground and the brand ink. Full-bleed background for heroes, page headers, the site header and footer, the education and newsletter bands, and every ad canvas in the Makerzoid precedent. On white surfaces it becomes the text colour for headings, product titles and labels. Translucent navy (85%) is used for the age badge on product photos and for bottom-up photo scrims.
- **Clean White** (#FFFFFF): The second ground, used interchangeably with navy. Product cards, testimonial cards, the "Why CREATESPACE" and final CTA sections, and the body of printed pages. On navy it is the text colour, often stepped down to 80%, 70% or 60% opacity for supporting lines.

### Secondary
- **Launch Orange** (#FF8B00): The action colour. Primary buttons, the ad CTA, inline "More about us" links, the header trust bar, the cart count, hover states on nav links and product titles, and regular (non-sale) prices. Also the most common single-word highlight in headlines ("Play." in the hero, `em` in the company profile) and the most common kicker colour.
- **Rocket Red** (#F70B28): The logomark "A" and the colour of savings. Sale pills in ads, discount percentage badges on product cards, the promo band badge, sale prices, error text and "Out of Stock". In the CI illustrations it is the giant "A".

### Tertiary
- **Sky Circuit Blue** (#3CC7F7): Cool counterweight to orange. The second headline highlight ("Learn." in the hero), kickers and labels on navy in print, the 9 to 12 age tile, icon discs, and the large blurred glow behind hero figures (25% opacity). Half of the promo band gradient.
- **Nebula Purple** (#AC4DFF): Secondary glow behind hero figures (20% opacity), the 13+ age tile, education kickers, and the other half of the promo band gradient.
- **Sprout Green** (#93DB21): "In Stock" indicator dot and text, the 6 to 8 age tile, and stat highlights in print. Pair with navy text when used as a fill.
- **Spark Yellow** (#FFD500): Rare and celebratory. Star ratings, and the "Sale" pill in the site navigation (yellow with navy text). Always carries navy text.
- **Chassis Grey** (#B3B3B3): The CI grey. On the website it appears only inside illustrations (robot bodies, wheels) and as the quiet quote mark on plain print testimonials. It is not used for text.

### Neutral
- **Workbench Mist** (#F9FAFB): Alternate section background on white pages (age groups, testimonials, photo wall) and the fill behind product photos on cards.
- **Hairline Grey** (#E5E7EB): Card borders, photo rings and dividers on the web.
- **Slate Body** (#4B5563): Supporting paragraph text on white (section subheadings, card descriptions, testimonial quotes at a slightly darker tone).
- **Caption Grey** (#6B7280): Captions, review counts, author roles, and struck-through compare-at prices (lighter still).
- **Print Muted Ink** (#4A5270): The navy-tinted body grey used in the A4 company profile instead of the web's cool slate. Prefer it for print.
- **Print Hairline** (#E4E7F0): The navy-tinted rule and card border colour in the company profile.

### Named Rules
**The Navy-or-White Ground Rule.** Every canvas grounds on Deep Space Navy or Clean White. Accent colours fill small shapes (buttons, badges, tiles, glows), never the whole canvas. The one sanctioned exception is the promo band's slow blue-to-purple gradient strip, which is a thin band, not a ground.

**The Orange Means Go Rule.** Launch Orange is the only fill for a primary call to action, on navy and on white alike. Secondary actions are outlined ghosts in navy or white, never a second accent colour.

**The Red Means Saving Rule.** In type and badges, Rocket Red signals a saving, a sale or urgency (discount badges, sale pills, sale prices, out of stock). Do not use red for decorative emphasis in headlines; that job belongs to orange and blue.

**The One-Word Highlight Rule.** Headlines are navy on white or white on navy, with at most one or two words picked out in an accent ("Build. **Play.** **Learn.**"). Highlight a word, never a whole line.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Outfit (with sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Outfit (with sans-serif)

**Character:** A single geometric sans with round, open letterforms that reads friendly and modern without looking childish. The CI specifies Outfit SemiBold for headlines and Outfit Regular for body copy and headlines; the website loads the variable font and uses heavier weights only for numbers and badges.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 72px desktop stepping to 60px and 48px on smaller screens, line-height 1.05, tracking -0.025em): The single h1 in heroes and page headers. Short, punchy, often three words with full stops ("Build. Play. Learn."). Balanced line wrapping.
- **Headline** (600, 36px desktop, 30px mobile, line-height about 1.2): Section headings ("Find the right kit", "A specialist store, not a toy aisle"). Sentence case.
- **Title** (600, 16px to 18px, line-height 1.375): Product card titles (clamped to two lines), feature point titles, footer column heads, button labels.
- **Lead** (400, 18px to 20px, line-height 1.625): Hero and page-header standfirst. On navy, the second sentence often drops to 60% white.
- **Body** (400, 16px, line-height 1.625): Paragraphs, capped at about 36rem to 42rem wide (roughly 60 to 70 characters).
- **Label** (500, 14px, uppercase, tracking 0.05em): Kickers above section headings ("THE NUMBERS DON'T LIE"), in orange, blue, purple or green. Print uses 600 weight at 6.6 to 6.8pt with 0.15em tracking.
- **Price** (700, 16px to 18px): Product prices. Orange by default, red when on sale, with the compare-at price struck through in light grey beside it.
- **Badge** (700 to 800, 11px to 14px): Discount and age badges, the promo band badge (uppercase, 800).
- **Sale numeral** (800, 190px on a 1080 canvas, line-height 0.85, tracking -8px): From the Makerzoid ad precedent. The giant "20%" in Rocket Red, followed by "OFF" at 700 weight and 78px with 0.05em tracking.

### Named Rules
**The SemiBold Ceiling Rule.** Words top out at SemiBold (600). Bold (700) and ExtraBold (800) are reserved for numbers and tiny labels: prices, discount percentages, stats, badges and the sale numeral.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Headlines, section headings and web buttons are sentence case. Uppercase is reserved for small tracked labels: kickers, badges, the sale pill and, in the ad precedent, the CTA.

## Layout

On the web, content sits in a centred container capped at 1280px with side gutters of 16px, 24px and 32px as the viewport widens. Sections stack as full-bleed bands that alternate navy, white and Workbench Mist, each with 64px to 80px of vertical padding (96px for the final CTA). Two-column splits (copy beside photo) collapse to a single column below 1024px. Grids of cards use 16px to 24px gaps; product grids run two columns on mobile.

Section openers are left-aligned (a headline, then a short grey subheading capped at about 36rem) except the closing call to action, which is centred. Hero copy sits in the left half with the photographic subject in the right half, grounded on the bottom edge.

**Translating to fixed canvases.** The precedents, not the website, set the rules here:
- **Outer margin:** the Makerzoid ads use 58px to 72px on a 1080px edge and the A4 company profile uses 13mm on a 210mm edge. Both land at roughly 5.5% to 6.5% of the short edge.
- **Square feed ad (1080 x 1080):** a 480px copy column on navy (logo top, message middle, CTA and URL bottom, spread with space-between) beside a photo panel that fades into navy from the left.
- **Story (1080 x 1920):** photo fills the top 58% with a navy gradient fading down into a solid navy copy block; sale pill top left, logo top right, CTA and URL on one row at the bottom.
- **Landscape link ad (1200 x 628):** the square layout compressed, with a 464px copy column.
- **A4 print:** navy header band with logo and headline, white body on a clear grid of cards and rules, navy footer band with contact details.

No precedent exists yet for 1080 x 1350 or A5 and A3; scale the square and A4 structures proportionally rather than inventing new ones.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat by default. Cards sit on white or mist with a thin grey border and no shadow at rest; on hover a product card gains a large, very faint navy shadow. Depth on navy comes from light, not shadow: big, heavily blurred discs of Sky Circuit Blue (25%) and Nebula Purple (20%) glow behind the hero figure and page-header art, and faint white dot grids or low-opacity circles of accent colour (10% to 14%) add texture in print and ads. Photos gain depth from navy scrims (navy at 85% fading to transparent) where text overlays them.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Resting lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`): Badges on photos and info badges.
- **Hover lift** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(12,20,70,0.05)`): Product card hover only.
- **Sale pill pop** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`): The yellow nav Sale pill.
- **Collage drop** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 48px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`): Rotated photos in the ad collage on navy.

### Named Rules
**The Glow Not Shadow Rule.** On navy, lift the subject with a soft blurred blue and purple glow behind it. Dark drop shadows are reserved for the rotated photo collage in ads.

## Shapes

Corners are consistently soft and friendly. Buttons and small tiles use 8px; product cards, photo frames and testimonial cards use 16px (12px on small screens); large panels such as the newsletter box use 24px. Anything that reads as a label (sale pill, discount and age badges, category chips, email field and subscribe button) is a full pill. The promo band badge is the one squarer label at 6px. In print, cards and photos use about 2.6mm; in ads, photos use 18px and the CTA 10px.

Borders are hairlines: 1px to 2px in Hairline Grey on white, or white at 10% to 30% on navy. Photos are clipped to their radius with a 1px ring. Circles appear as icon discs (navy or blue at 5% to 10% tint), portrait crops with a 0.7mm accent ring in print, and glows.

## Components

### Buttons
Warm, confident and chunky; they look pressable.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px).
- **Primary:** Launch Orange fill, white SemiBold label, 16px by 32px padding, sentence case ("Browse the range"), optional right arrow icon.
- **Hover / Focus:** fill eases to 90% orange; pressing nudges it down 1px.
- **Ghost on navy:** transparent with a 1px white-at-30% border and white label; hover adds a 10% white wash.
- **Ghost on white:** transparent with a 1px navy-at-20% border and navy label; hover adds a 5% navy wash.
- **Ad CTA (precedent):** Launch Orange, white 700 label, uppercase with 0.08em tracking, 18px by 42px at 26px type on a 1080 canvas, 10px radius. Always paired with the URL "thecreatespace.co.za" in white at 30%.

### Chips
- **Category chip:** white pill with a 1px Hairline Grey border, navy 500 label, and a round Workbench Mist disc holding a small product or illustration icon on the left. Hover switches the border to orange.
- **Skill tags:** outlined pills in 14px 500 type.

### Price and Sale Badges
- **Sale pill (ads):** Rocket Red full pill, white 700 uppercase label with 0.14em tracking and a small white dot before it ("LIMITED TIME SALE").
- **Discount badge (cards):** Rocket Red full pill, white 700 "-20%", top left of the product photo.
- **Age badge (cards):** navy at 85% full pill, white 600 "Ages 8-12", top right of the product photo.
- **Promo band badge:** Rocket Red, 6px radius, white 800 uppercase, sitting on the blue-to-purple band beside a navy eyebrow pill and a navy SemiBold message.
- **Nav Sale pill:** Spark Yellow full pill, navy 700 label, small dark drop shadow.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px (12px on mobile).
- **Background:** Clean White, with the product photo on Workbench Mist in a square frame.
- **Shadow Strategy:** flat at rest; faint navy shadow on hover (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 2px Hairline Grey (lighter tint) on product cards; testimonial cards are borderless white on mist.
- **Internal Padding:** 20px (12px on mobile) for product cards, 24px to 32px for testimonials.

### Product Card
The unit that reappears in ads and catalogues. Square photo with the discount badge top left and age badge top right; below it the SemiBold navy title (two lines max), a coloured stock dot and label (green In Stock, orange lead time, red Out of Stock), star rating in Spark Yellow with a grey count, and a bottom row with the price (orange, or red with a struck-through compare-at price) and an Add to Cart button.

### Section Kicker and Heading
A small uppercase kicker in an accent colour (orange by default, blue or purple for education) sits 8px above a SemiBold headline, followed by a Slate Body subheading. In print the kicker shrinks to about 6.6pt with wide 0.15em tracking.

### Logo Lockup
- **Versions:** white wordmark with red "A" on navy; navy wordmark with red "A" on white; mono white on black; mono black on white (CI page 5). In the marketing kit these are `creative/kit/logo/logo-on-navy.png` and `logo-on-white.png`. (On the website the navy-background version is confusingly named `logo-dark.png`.)
- **Clear space:** at least "x" on every side, where x is the height of the logomark (CI page 6).
- **Sizes in use:** 24px tall in the site header, 28px in the footer, 7.8mm in the A4 header, 46px to 58px on 1080px ad canvases. The CI gives no minimum size.
- **Logomark alone:** the red "A" on navy is a sanctioned standalone mark (CI page 4).

### Illustration Usage
The brand illustrations are outlined, flat-coloured STEM objects (robots, planets, atoms, beakers, microscopes, nuts, chips, code brackets) in the accent palette with black strokes.
- **Background decor (web default):** one or two per section, 5% to 8% opacity, rotated 3 to 12 degrees, tucked into corners and partly cropped by the edge, hidden on small screens.
- **Hero orbit:** up to three full-colour pieces (planets, atom) orbiting the photographic subject, some partly behind it, one at 60% opacity.
- **CI compositions:** on navy grounds the CI stacks full-colour pieces along the bottom edge and hangs planets from the top, leaving the centre clear for a headline. Treat this as the model for poster-style illustration layouts.
- **Print:** a single robot anchored in the header corner; a cog at 7% opacity, whitened, in the footer.

### Photo Treatment
Real photographs of children and hands building kits. Frames use 16px radius with a 1px ring. Text over photos sits on a navy scrim rising from the bottom (85% to transparent). In ads, photos either fade into the navy copy panel with a horizontal navy gradient or scatter as a collage of two or three photos rotated between -2.5 and 3 degrees with 18px corners and a deep shadow, with soft orange and blue circles behind. Hero figures are cut-outs grounded on the bottom edge with a blue and purple glow behind.

### Navigation (web only)
Sticky navy bar with an orange trust strip above it, the white logo left, white 14px links that turn orange on hover, and the yellow Sale pill. Not relevant to fixed-canvas assets beyond the orange trust strip, which can be quoted as a thin band of promises.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** ground every asset on Deep Space Navy or Clean White, and let the accents appear as small, bright shapes.
- **Do** make every primary call to action Launch Orange with white SemiBold text.
- **Do** reserve Rocket Red for sale pills, discount badges and sale prices, and show the saving as a big numeral when it is the message.
- **Do** highlight one or two words of a headline in orange or blue, keeping the rest navy or white.
- **Do** keep headlines at Outfit SemiBold (600) in sentence case; save 700 and 800 for numbers and badges.
- **Do** keep clear space of at least the logomark height around the logo, and use the white-wordmark version on navy and the navy-wordmark version on white.
- **Do** use real photos of children and hands building kits as the emotional lead, with navy scrims for any text on top.
- **Do** lift subjects on navy with a soft blurred blue and purple glow.
- **Do** use rounded corners throughout: 8px buttons, 16px photos and cards, full pills for labels.
- **Do** keep outer margins at about 6% of the short edge on fixed canvases, as the precedents do.

### Don't:
- **Don't** flood a canvas with an accent colour; the only sanctioned accent ground is the thin blue-to-purple promo band.
- **Don't** use a second accent colour for a primary button, or make secondary actions filled.
- **Don't** use red for decorative headline emphasis.
- **Don't** set headlines in Bold or ExtraBold, or in all caps.
- **Don't** let illustrations compete with the product photo or the message: faint in the background, or a few full-colour pieces orbiting the subject, never both at full strength behind text.
- **Don't** use Chassis Grey or accent colours for body text; body text is navy, Slate Body grey on white, or white at reduced opacity on navy.
- **Don't** add heavy dark drop shadows to cards; depth comes from glows, scrims and the ad collage only.
- **Don't** place the colour logo on anything other than navy or white; the CI shows only navy, white, and mono on black or white.
