## The one thing

Both microscopes are great, they just suit different kids. The MX2-AS is the little explorer's microscope that goes outside; the MT3-2 is a proper lab microscope for older kids who want to see cells. A parent should be able to look at the post and know which one fits their child.

The post compares fairly. Neither is "the better one", and the cheaper one is not framed as the lesser one.
