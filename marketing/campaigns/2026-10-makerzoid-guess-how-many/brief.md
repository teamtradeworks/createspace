# Guess how many (Ad brief C2)

## Summary

- **Campaign slug:** `2026-10-makerzoid-guess-how-many` (also the `utm_campaign` value)
- **Type:** Product
- **Audience:** Parents
- **Runs:** not scheduled. Guess post first, reveal post on the Friday after.
- **Status:** Draft

## The one thing

An easy guessing game that gets parents commenting: "How many robots are in this box?" The reveal post answers it: 200+ robot models from one Robot Master Premium.

## Look

Pinned by the brief and deliberately louder than the usual navy/white system: a solid Spark Yellow `#FFD500` ground with navy text, and an Outfit ExtraBold (800) headline at 104px. Outfit ExtraBold was added to the kit (`creative/kit/fonts/Outfit-ExtraBold.ttf`, weight 800 in `brand.css`) for this.

- Wordmark top-left (`logo-on-white.png`, navy with the red A).
- The box on a white card tilted -3 degrees, sitting low and large.
- A navy disc with a white "?" on the card's top-right corner, tilted 10 degrees on the page.
- A navy rule over the footer: product name left, "Hint: it's not 10." right.
- No CTA button, price or age on the image: this is a game, not a product ad. Meta supplies the button.

**The box spoils the answer.** The box art prints "200+ IN 1" under "PREMIUM". On the guess post a strip of masking tape reading "No peeking!" covers it. On the reveal post the tape is moved to the card's corner and a hand-drawn orange arrow points at the printed "200+ IN 1".
