**Watch:**
- **Age.** content.md records the Shopify metafield as 8+ and notes some retailers list 6+. The ad says 6-12, matching the brief and the hero kits ads. Confirm the Shopify age metafield agrees before upload, or change the footer.
- **"30 metres"** in the second quote is the box's "up to 100 feet". It's the child's boast, so it's fine without "up to", but don't turn it into a guaranteed height in the primary text.
- **The quotes are illustrative**, not from a real customer. Don't present them as a testimonial.
- **Price** is left off the image to keep the native look; it goes in the primary text.

## Licensor review (National Geographic)

National Geographic is a licensed brand, so this ad goes to the licensor before it runs.
- **Imagery:** the only product image is the official box front from the Shopify listing (`assets/product/national-geographic-light-up-air-rockets/shopify/cover-image.jpg`), cropped to the box and resized. It is not edited, recoloured or composited.
- **Logo:** the National Geographic yellow border appears only as printed on the box. We don't reproduce it anywhere else.
- **Copy to clear:** the humorous quotes, especially "Please don't launch rockets at the dog." Ask whether a joke about pointing rockets at a pet conflicts with the box's safety warnings or brand guidelines. Fallback line if it's refused: "Please don't launch rockets at your brother."
- **Name:** the footer shortens the product to "Light-Up Air Rockets". Confirm the licensor accepts that without "National Geographic" in the line (the box shows the full name).
