# Parenting Hub MREC (brand awareness)

## Summary

- **Campaign slug:** `2026-10-parenting-hub-mrec` (also the `utm_campaign` value)
- **Type:** CREATESPACE awareness
- **Audience:** Parents reading Parenting Hub
- **Placement:** 300 x 250 display banner (MREC) on parentinghub.co.za
- **Status:** Draft

## The one thing

Replace the underperforming Parenting Hub banner. It sits among many other ads, so it has to pop and make a parent curious enough to tap. The banner names no product; the microscope is there as the picture.

## Look

Built on the structure of CREATESPACE's earlier clean MRECs (photo on the left, logo, "Build. Play. Learn." and a short line on the right), on Deep Space Navy rather than white, made more fun:

- **Navy, not white:** the copy panel is Deep Space Navy with white text and the white wordmark logo. "Play." sits on a tilted Sky Circuit Blue marker, echoing the blue "Play." of the earlier banners. The supporting line is white at 85%.
- **More fun:** a smooth three-curve wave where the photo meets the navy; the plant cell from her microscope screen pops out under a magnifier (white ring, navy handle); a CI atom illustration peeks in from the right edge.
- **A button:** an orange "Shop now" with a navy label replaces the plain URL line, so the click target is obvious.

A 1px navy edge keeps the banner from merging into a white web page.

## Copy

- **Headline:** Build. Play. Learn.
- **Line:** Hands-on STEM kits that turn playtime into discovery.
- **CTA:** Shop now
