# Venn meme (Ad brief N1)

## Summary

- **Campaign slug:** `2026-10-venn-meme` (also the `utm_campaign` value)
- **Type:** Product
- **Audience:** Parents
- **Runs:** not scheduled
- **Status:** Draft

## The one thing

Stop the scroll with a parent meme everyone recognises (the quiet, screen-free, useful Saturday morning), then show the kit as the answer.

## Look

This set is deliberately off-system. The brief asks for something that looks like a home-made meme a parent would share, not a designed ad, so it uses DM Sans (`creative/kit/fonts/DMSans.ttf`, OFL) and black text on off-white `#FBFAF6` instead of Outfit on navy. The only brand elements are the Venn fills (CI blue, yellow and green at 30%) and the small wordmark. No CTA on the image.
