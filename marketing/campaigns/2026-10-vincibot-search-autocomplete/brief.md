## The one thing

Mirror the exact thing a parent types into a search bar, then answer it with the kit.

## Look

Deliberately partly off-system. The top half has to read as the parent's own phone, so the search panel is generic: grey ground `#F3F4F7`, a white sheet with a soft shadow, DM Sans (`creative/kit/fonts/DMSans.ttf`, as in the 2026-10 Venn meme) and no search engine branding. The answer card switches to CREATESPACE: Outfit on navy, blue label, yellow age highlight.

The CTA is a **red** pill because the brief asks for it. That breaks DESIGN.md's "Orange Means Go" rule (red is meant for sales). White on Rocket Red is about 4.2:1, which passes AA for large text at 31px SemiBold.

**Watch:**
- **"…without a tablet"** is an autocomplete suggestion, not the query the card answers, but a parent may read "Found it" as answering it too. That's only partly true. The three preset modes (line following, drawing, IR remote) need no device, but coding and the 75 activities run in the MatataCode app on a tablet or computer. Keep the primary text from promising screen-free play.
- **"75 activity cards"** follows MatataStudio's own wording. The activities are online, so don't say they're printed.
