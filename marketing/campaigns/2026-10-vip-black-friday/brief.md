# VIP Black Friday: subscribe for early access

## Summary

- **Campaign slug:** `2026-10-vip-black-friday` (also the `utm_campaign` value)
- **Type:** Sale (pre-sale list building)
- **Audience:** Parents
- **Persona:** Gift-buying parents and grandparents planning ahead for the festive season (`assets/brand/brand-strategy.md`)
- **Runs:** Late October 2026 until the VIP early-access sale opens
- **Owner:** Hayley
- **Status:** Draft

## The one thing

Join the CREATESPACE email list now, because subscribers get into the Black Friday sale before it opens to everyone else.

## Subject

**Sale:** A VIP early-access Black Friday sale for email subscribers only. No discount, start date or product list is confirmed yet, so the assets promise early access and nothing more.

### Lock screen variant (`feed-square-notification`, `feed-portrait-notification`, `story-notification`)

Added 2026-10-28 as a challenger to the collage square. A parent's phone lock screen dated Thursday 19 November (the day VIP opens), their kids as the wallpaper (`dad-and-kids-launching-rockets.jpg`), showing the email subscribers will get: "You're in early for Black Friday" with a red VIP stamp ("Subscribers shop our Black Friday sale from 19 to 22 Nov. Everyone else waits until the 23rd."), and a message from Gran underneath ("Have you sorted the kids' presents yet?? x"). The lock screen is generic (DM Sans, no OS branding), like the Notes and search ads. Bottom strip: "Sign up now to shop 4 days early." / "Get on the list". No logo (removed by the owner 2026-10-28); the CREATESPACE notification carries the brand.

The campaign runs before the sale opens, so the lock screen is dated 19 November to show it as a future moment, and the notification gives dates instead of saying the sale "is open". Primary text for this variant: "Our Black Friday sale opens to everyone on 23 November. Subscribers get in four days earlier, from 19 to 22 November. Sign up now and this lands on your phone before anyone else's. It's free and takes ten seconds."

Why this style: it shows the reward itself (being first to hear) instead of describing it, it looks like phone content rather than an ad, and Gran's message adds gift-buying timing without inventing a deadline. Gran is made up; don't present her as a real customer.

### Struck-through variant (`feed-portrait-struck.html`, `feed-square-struck.html`, `story-struck.html`, added 2026-11-02)

Built on `2026-10-makerzoid-72-robots`, the best performer in the test campaign, using the same layout and product photo: white ground, one struck-through "old way" line (faded grey, red strike), a 112px navy headline with one number on a yellow marker, the Makerzoid box and builds on a pale blue card bleeding off the right edge, and a red CTA pill. A round red stamp on the card ("VIP from 19 Nov") carries the date. One line under the headline says who it's for, so the headline isn't read as a sale open to everyone. A first version with a calendar card and a body paragraph was dropped on 2026-11-02 as too wordy and plain.

The three sizes share `struck.css`, and each file sets only its own sizes. The square shrinks everything a step, and its card is a 620px block inside the margins instead of bleeding off the edge (a full-width card on the short canvas read as a long thin strip), with the stamp beside it overlapping the card's right edge. The story keeps the portrait layout between the top and bottom 270px, and moves the stamp to the bottom right of the card because the taller photo's builds column reaches the top right.

- **Struck:** "Wait for Black Friday like everyone else."
- **Headline:** "Shop Black Friday 4 days early." (the 4 is marked)
- **Under the headline:** "Only for people on our email list."
- **Stamp:** "VIP from 19 Nov"
- **Footer:** "VIP early access, 19 to 22 Nov" / thecreatespace.co.za / button "Sign up free"

Image: `images/makerzoid-smart-robot-box-and-builds.jpg`, copied from the 72 robots campaign (source `assets/product/makerzoid-smart-robot/lifestyle/whats-in-the-box.jpg`). It shows the kind of thing on offer; the ad doesn't claim the Makerzoid is in the sale.

Like the 72 robots ad, it uses red for the strike and the CTA instead of orange (Orange Means Go). Here red stands for urgency (the early-access date), which the Red Means Saving rule allows, and it keeps the look of the winning ad.

## Constraints

- No discount on the image until the owner confirms it. Dates are confirmed (2026-10-28): VIP 19 to 22 Nov, open sale 23 to 30 Nov.

## Open questions

- **The landing page doesn't mention VIP access yet.** The homepage popup and newsletter band both say "Stay in the loop". Someone tapping this ad should see "Get VIP early access to Black Friday" where they sign up, or the ad's promise gets lost. Update `EmailPopup.tsx` and `NewsletterBand.tsx` copy before the ad goes live (or consider a Meta Instant Form).
- **The popup never opens by itself.** `EmailPopup.tsx` only opens on an `open-email-popup` event, so ad traffic lands on the homepage and has to scroll to the newsletter band near the bottom. Either open the popup for visitors arriving with `utm_campaign=2026-10-vip-black-friday`, or point the ad at a short dedicated VIP signup page.
- What's the discount? Add it once confirmed.
- The collage `feed-square`, `feed-portrait` and `story` still carry no dates. Consider adding "19 to 22 Nov" now that they're confirmed.
