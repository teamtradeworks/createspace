## The one thing

Robot Master Premium is not a one-build toy. A parent's own holiday list shows four builds done and "about 196 more to go".

## Look

Deliberately partly off-system, like the 2026-10 VinciBot search ad. The note has to read as the parent's own phone, so it is a generic notes look, not a copy of any real app: cream paper `#FFFDF3`, DM Sans (`creative/kit/fonts/DMSans.ttf`), a mustard tint (`#D6A21C` for ticks and caret, `#A77A06` for the "Notes" label so it clears 3:1 as large text), grey struck-through items and the open item in near-black with a text caret after it.

CREATESPACE appears only in the stuck-on photo of the box (white card, tilted -4 degrees, a strip of tape) and the small wordmark bottom left. There is no CTA button on the image; Meta supplies the button.

**Watch:**
- **The four builds are illustrative**, not a list from a real customer. They stay generic (a robot dog, a crane, a walking dinosaur, a coded car) and match the kinds of models the kit makes (content.md mentions dinosaurs, cranes, cars). Liam is a made-up child. Don't present the note as a testimonial in the primary text.
- **"About 196 more to go"** is 200 minus the four ticked builds. It follows from the 200+ claim, so keep "about".
- The box art says "6+"; the age range we sell on is 6-14.
