## Description

Mastered Kanoodle? It's time for **Educational Insights Kanoodle Genius**, the advanced version of the pocket-sized brain teaser. Pick a puzzle from the guide, set up the pieces shown, then work out how to fit the rest. Every puzzle has just one solution.

The puzzles come as flat 2D boards and 3D pyramids, and start where the original Kanoodle leaves off. They get harder with every level, so they'll stump plenty of grown-ups too. The case holds everything, so it's ideal for road trips, holidays and quiet time.

**Key Features**
- 204 advanced puzzles, harder than the original Kanoodle
- Flat 2D puzzles and 3D pyramid puzzles
- Difficulty climbs with every level
- Each puzzle has just one solution
- Builds spatial reasoning, logic and problem-solving skills
- Pocket-sized case doubles as the game board and stores every piece
- Single-player and screen-free, great for travel
- No batteries needed
- Recommended age range: 8+ years

**Batteries required:** None

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years.
