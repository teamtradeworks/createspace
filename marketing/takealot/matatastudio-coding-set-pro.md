## Description

The **MatataStudio Coding Set Pro** teaches real coding with no screen, app or reading needed. Kids line up coding blocks marked with arrows on a board, the tower's camera reads them, and the little MatataBot robot drives off to follow their code.

Three challenge booklets take kids from first forward and turn moves to loops and functions, the same ideas programmers use every day. They plan routes across the map, steer around obstacles and get MatataBot to play music and dance. When the robot goes the wrong way, kids swap a block and try again.

**Key Features**
- No screen, app, internet or reading needed
- 37 coding blocks: motion, loop, function, number and fun blocks
- The tower's camera reads the code, and MatataBot follows it straight away
- 3 challenge booklets, beginner to advanced, with 100+ stories and challenges
- Teaches sequencing, loops, functions and debugging
- Fun blocks make MatataBot move at random, play music and dance
- Two-sided map, obstacles and flags for building coding challenges
- LEGO-compatible robot for custom builds
- Robot and tower are rechargeable, with about 5 hours of play per charge
- Works with the Musician, Artist and Animation add-ons (sold separately)
- Builds logic, problem solving and early maths skills
- Recommended age range: 4+ years

**Batteries required:** Built-in rechargeable lithium batteries in the robot and tower (included). Charge via USB-C.

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years. Contains lithium batteries. Adult supervision recommended when charging.

## Notes

- The old listing said "Scratch compatible". The research says this set is screen-free and app-free, with no Scratch support found, so it's left out.
- The old listing said ages 5-9. The manufacturer says 4 to 9, so the listing uses 4+.
- Our product page says the Pro set has music blocks and angle blocks for drawing. The research's 37-block breakdown has neither (music comes from a "preset music" fun block). Drawing and music blocks are left out until a unit from stock confirms them.
- The coding blocks' material isn't confirmed (some older Matatalab sets used wooden blocks), so the copy doesn't describe them.
- The listing says it contains lithium batteries, but Takealot's dangerous goods rules weren't checked. Check them before submitting.
