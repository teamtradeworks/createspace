## Description

The **MatataStudio MT3-2** is a real lab-style microscope with a built-in camera and a 7-inch screen in place of the eyepiece. Three objective lenses give up to 400x optical magnification, and a metal stage with adjustment knobs lets kids move slides with precision, just like in a school lab.

Kids start with the 10 prepared slides, then use the slide kit to make their own from leaves, onion skin or pond water. They can take photos and videos, measure specimens on screen, and stream the live view to a phone or tablet over Wi-Fi.

**Key Features**
- 3 objective lenses (4x, 10x and 40x) for 40x to 400x optical magnification
- 7-inch screen that tilts 135°, so a whole group can look at once
- Built-in 2MP camera for photos and videos, saved to the included 8GB SD card
- Metal X-Y stage with coaxial knobs for smooth, precise slide movement
- Two adjustable LED lights: from above for solid objects, from below for slides
- On-screen measuring tool, with a calibration ruler included
- 10 prepared slides, plus a slide kit for making your own
- Streams to phones and tablets over Wi-Fi, or connects to a computer with USB-C
- Free MatataXplore app for iOS, Android, Windows and Mac
- Rechargeable battery with 2.5 to 3 hours of use per charge
- Recommended age range: 11+ years

**Batteries required:** Built-in rechargeable lithium battery (included). Charges via USB-C.

**Warning:** Contains glass slides and small parts. Not suitable for children under 3 years. Contains a lithium battery. Adult supervision recommended when charging.

## Notes

- The old listing said "optical magnification up to 400x" with "continuous zoom". The MT3-2 changes magnification by switching between 3 lenses, not continuous zoom, so continuous zoom is left out.
- The old listing had the lights the wrong way round. Light from above is for solid objects; light from below shines through slides.
- HDMI output appears in our Shopify copy and on our product page but not in the manufacturer specs we checked. It may belong to the MT3-4 or MT3-5. It's left out until a unit from stock confirms an HDMI port.
- "Intelligent imaging" is left out. No source says what it does on this model.
- The 10 prepared slides aren't named in any source (described only as "plant tissue to everyday materials"). Check the box before naming them.
- Slide kit contents aren't listed in any source. Check before describing the tools.
- The listing says it contains a lithium battery, but Takealot's dangerous goods rules weren't checked. Check them before submitting.
