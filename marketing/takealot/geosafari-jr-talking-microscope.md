## Description

The **Educational Insights GeoSafari Jr. Talking Microscope** brings science to life for little ones. Kids slide in a picture, peek through the two eyepieces and hear fun facts about animals, plants and everyday objects, read by wildlife conservationist Bindi Irwin.

There's no focusing and no need to close one eye, so even young children can explore on their own. Fact Mode teaches them about each picture, then Quiz Mode tests what they remember, building curiosity, new words and a love of science.

**Key Features**
- Talking microscope with 5x magnification
- 20 slides with 60 close-up photos of animals, plants and household items
- Over 100 facts and quiz questions, read by Bindi Irwin
- Two modes: Fact Mode and Quiz Mode
- Two eyepieces and no focusing needed, made for small children
- Choose English, Spanish, French or German audio
- Slides store neatly in a drawer inside the microscope
- Recommended age range: 3+ years

**Batteries required:** 3 x AAA (not included)

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years.
