## Description

The **Educational Insights GeoSafari Jr. Kidnoculars** are binoculars made just for little hands and little faces. The extra-large, goggle-style eyepieces rest easily against a child's face, so they can see straight away without squinting or fiddling with a focus knob.

Take them to the garden, the park, on holiday or on a game drive. Kids can spot birds, bugs, trees and the moon, and with 2x magnification and no batteries or screens, they're a simple way to get young explorers outside and curious about nature.

**Key Features**
- Kids binoculars designed for small children
- Gentle 2x magnification, with no focusing needed
- Extra-large, goggle-style eyepieces that are easy for toddlers to use
- Breakaway neck strap for safe, easy carrying
- Sturdy, lightweight plastic built for outdoor play
- Encourages bird watching, nature exploration and observation skills
- No batteries or screens needed
- Recommended age range: 3+ years

**Batteries required:** None

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years. Do not look at the sun through the binoculars.
