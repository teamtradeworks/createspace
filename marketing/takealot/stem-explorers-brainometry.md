## Description

**Learning Resources STEM Explorers Brainometry** is a hands-on shape puzzle that gets kids thinking in 3D. Pick a challenge card, gather the pieces shown, then work out how to build the shape, like a flat triangle, a standing arch or a house with a pointy roof.

The challenges start simple and get trickier, building spatial reasoning, problem solving and early maths skills along the way. Once kids have solved all 10, they can invent their own puzzles and buildings.

**Key Features**
- 24 chunky 3D shape pieces in 4 colours and fun patterns
- 6 different shapes: L, T, zigzag, cross, cube and triangle
- 10 challenge cards that go from beginner to expert
- Builds spatial reasoning, problem solving and fine motor skills
- Introduces shapes, patterns and early geometry
- Single-player and screen-free, great for quiet time or travel
- No batteries needed
- Recommended age range: 5+ years

**Batteries required:** None

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years.
