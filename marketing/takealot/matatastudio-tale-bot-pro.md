## Description

The **MatataStudio Tale-Bot Pro** is a friendly little robot that teaches coding before kids can even read. Children press the arrow buttons on its back to plan a route, tap go, and watch Tale-Bot walk it. No tablet or screen needed.

Tale-Bot also draws with washable markers, sings, dances and plays back stories kids record themselves. Themed maps, such as a frog's life cycle, turn each route into an adventure, and 14 missions in the booklet build skills step by step, from first sequences to loops.

**Key Features**
- Code with the arrow buttons on the robot, with no screen or reading needed
- Coloured lights show each step as it runs, so kids can spot and fix mistakes
- Draws shapes with 2 washable markers, and sings and dances too
- Built-in microphone records stories of up to 30 seconds
- 5 double-sided themed maps, a blank map and 32 command cards
- Challenge booklet with 14 missions
- Swappable wings and arms, including arms that fit LEGO and DUPLO bricks
- Speaks 11 languages, including English
- Optional Scratch coding on a tablet or computer as kids grow
- Rechargeable battery with up to 4 hours of play per charge
- Builds sequencing, logic, problem solving and storytelling
- Recommended age range: 3+ years

**Batteries required:** Built-in rechargeable lithium battery (included). Charges via USB-C.

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years. Contains a lithium battery. Adult supervision recommended when charging.
