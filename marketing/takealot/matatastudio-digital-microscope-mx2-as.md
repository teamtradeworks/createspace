## Description

The **MatataStudio MX2-AS** is a digital microscope that kids can carry into the garden or clip onto its stand at a desk. There's no eyepiece to squint through: everything shows up on its own tiltable screen, so even young scientists can see what they're looking at straight away.

Kids zoom in on leaves, bugs, sugar crystals and the 5 prepared slides in the box, then tap to take photos or videos of what they find. Connect it to a phone, tablet or computer with the free MatataXplore app to see discoveries on a bigger screen and share them.

**Key Features**
- 2-in-1: handheld for exploring outdoors, or clipped onto its stand for slides
- 4.3-inch built-in screen that tilts 135° for comfortable viewing
- Smooth, continuous zoom up to 1600x digital magnification
- Two LED lights: from above for solid objects, from below for slides
- Touch buttons to take photos and videos, saved to the included 8GB microSD card
- Connects to phones and tablets over Wi-Fi, or to a computer with USB-C
- Free MatataXplore app for iOS, Android, Windows, Mac and Chromebook
- Rechargeable battery with about 3 hours of use per charge
- Builds observation skills, curiosity and early science knowledge
- Recommended age range: 5+ years

**Batteries required:** Built-in rechargeable lithium battery (included). Charges via USB-C.

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years. Contains a lithium battery. Adult supervision recommended when charging.

## Notes

- The old listing said "7-inch" screen. The screen is 4.3 inches, per the manufacturer and our product page.
- The old listing had the lights the wrong way round. Light from above is for solid objects; light from below shines through slides.
- The old listing said "0.5x-4x optical magnification". That figure came from our old Shopify copy and doesn't match the manufacturer (9x to 72x optical, 100x to 1600x digital). The description uses the 1600x digital figure only.
- "Intelligent dyeing" (automatic colour enhancement) is left out because shoppers won't know the term.
- Age: the old listing said 4 to 11. The manufacturer says 5 to 12, so the listing uses 5+.
- The 5 prepared slides are not named in any source. Check the box before naming them.
