## Description

The **Learning Resources Gears! Gears! Gears! Deluxe Building Set** is a hands-on building toy that shows little ones how things move. Kids snap colourful gears onto bases, build towers with pillars and axles, then turn the crank and watch their whole creation spin.

Along the way they learn simple engineering ideas, like how one gear turns the next, and build finger strength, problem-solving and early maths skills. It's open-ended, so it keeps up with your child as they grow, and it works with all other Gears! Gears! Gears! sets.

**Key Features**
- 100-piece gear building set
- Crank handle sets the whole build moving at once
- Build flat, tall and sideways for hundreds of designs
- Introduces cause and effect and early engineering
- Builds fine motor skills and spatial thinking
- Activity guide with simple builds and experiments
- No batteries or screens needed
- Recommended age range: 3+ years

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years.
