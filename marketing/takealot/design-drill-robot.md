## Description

The **Educational Insights Design & Drill Robot** is a take-apart robot that little builders put together themselves. Kids snap on the arms, use the kid-sized screwdriver to twist in 15 colourful bolts, then decorate their robot with stickers.

Once it's built, the robot's head, arms and upper body swivel, so it's ready for play. Kids can take it apart and build it again and again, strengthening little fingers and hand-eye coordination and getting a first taste of engineering.

**Key Features**
- Take-apart robot (about 20 cm tall) that kids build themselves
- Easy-grip screwdriver sized for small hands
- 15 colourful bolts for matching, sorting and patterns
- Sticker sheet to decorate and personalise the robot
- Swivelling head, arms and upper body for play once built
- Builds fine motor skills, problem solving and early engineering
- No batteries or screens needed
- Recommended age range: 3+ years

**Batteries required:** None

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years.
