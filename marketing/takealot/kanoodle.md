## Description

**Educational Insights Kanoodle** is a pocket-sized brain teaser with puzzles that start easy and get really tricky. Pick a puzzle from the book, set up the pieces shown, then work out how to fit the rest in. There are hundreds of ways to try, but only one solution.

Puzzles come in two styles, flat 2D boards and 3D pyramids, and get harder level by level, so kids can start easy and work up to puzzles that challenge adults too. Everything packs into its own case, so it's great for car trips, holidays and waiting rooms.

**Key Features**
- 228 puzzles, from beginner to expert
- Flat 2D puzzles and 3D pyramid puzzles
- Each puzzle has just one solution
- Builds spatial reasoning, logic and problem-solving skills
- Pocket-sized case doubles as the game board and stores every piece
- Single-player and screen-free, great for travel
- No batteries needed
- Recommended age range: 7+ years

**Batteries required:** None

**Warning:** Choking hazard. Small parts. Not for children under 3 years.
