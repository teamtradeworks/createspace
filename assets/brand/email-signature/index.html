Add it to Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, open the gear icon, then See all settings. Stay on the General tab and scroll to Signature.
  3. Click Create new, give it a name, click once inside the editor and paste (Cmd+V or Ctrl+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and for replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom of the page.
