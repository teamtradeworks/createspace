Our vision

To ignite a passion for science, technology, engineering and mathematics.

Our mission

To put well-made STEM products and programmes in the hands of young learners. Children who meet science and engineering early, through play, stay curious about how things work. STEM is for everyone, whatever their background or ability.

For families

Every product lists its age range, skill level and what a child will learn, so a kit can be matched to the child who will build it.

For schools

Trained facilitators, CAPS-aligned lesson plans and classroom kits built for group work, with bulk pricing for schools from 10 units.

Every order is picked and packed by hand in our Cape Town warehouse.
