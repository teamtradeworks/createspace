### Product Overview
The Makerzoid Robot Master Premium is a comprehensive STEAM robot building kit designed by robotics experts. It combines physical construction with digital programming, enabling children to build over 200 different robot models while learning fundamental engineering and coding concepts.

The kit uses Scratch 3.0, a visual block-based programming language specifically designed for children. Through drag-and-drop programming, children can control their robots, program movements, and create interactive projects without needing to learn complex coding syntax.

## Learning Outcomes (Expanded)

### Why Hands-On Learning Works

**Research Evidence:**

Research from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln demonstrates that students who engage in hands-on learning retain **50% more information** than peers who learn through traditional lectures.

A landmark study tracking 862 elementary students (ages 7-10) through the Robotics and Coding Academy showed remarkable measurable outcomes:
- **89% gains in coding skills** (from 3% pre-program to 92% post-program, p < 0.001)
- **67% gains in robotic design knowledge** (from 26% pre to 93% post, p < 0.01)
- **32% increase in STEM career interest** (p < 0.05)

The study also found that "the amount of time students spend in the program is positively correlated with their increases in coding knowledge" - meaning longer engagement produces better results.
