## Learning Outcomes (Expanded)

### Why Imaginative Role Play Works

Research from the Child Mind Institute and the Early Childhood Research & Practice Journal shows that pretend play is one of the most powerful tools for child development:

- **Theory of Mind:** Children who engage in pretend play develop earlier understanding that others hold different beliefs and perspectives — a foundational social skill
- **Emotion Regulation:** "Kids who engage more in these kinds of play have stronger emotion knowledge and emotion regulation and control" (Child Mind Institute)
- **Executive Function:** Role-play strengthens the ability to follow rules, maintain goals, and manage cognitive flexibility — essential for academic success
- **Language Development:** Children whose imaginative play involves elaborate storytelling demonstrate more advanced vocabularies and improved narrative abilities
- **Self-Regulation:** Research shows "make-believe play serves as a vital context for the development of self-regulation at the preschool level" (ECRP, University of Illinois)
- **Problem-Solving:** Meta-analyses indicate sociodramatic play effectively promotes problem-solving abilities and divergent thinking

Source: https://childmind.org/article/the-power-of-pretend-play-for-children/ and https://ecrp.illinois.edu/v4n1/bergen.html

### Why Space and Astronomy Spark STEM Interest

Research shows that astronomy is uniquely powerful for sparking lifelong STEM curiosity in children:

- Astronomy naturally encourages children to ask "why" and "how" questions — the foundation of scientific inquiry
- Children who engage with space science develop technological literacy and understanding of exploration tools
- Early exposure to astronomy correlates with sustained STEM engagement as children grow older
- Space themes motivate children to observe, sort, predict and hypothesise — core scientific process skills

Source: https://stellarinspire.com/blog/why-astronomy-education-important-children and https://www.telescopeguide.org/astronomy-for-kids/
